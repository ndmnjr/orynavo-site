Privacy

How this site handles your privacy.

This is an informational website. Orynavo does not use tracking tools to collect visitor data.

When you visit

This site uses no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. It has no account, newsletter or contact forms. Orynavo does not receive personal information just because you visit.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may process routine technical logs, including your IP address, browser information and request time, to keep the service running and secure. You can read more in GitHub's privacy statement.

When you send an email

If you email Orynavo, including hello@orynavo.com or research@orynavo.com, Orynavo receives what you choose to include. That may be your name, contact details, role, organisation and message.

Orynavo uses that information to reply, hold a research conversation you requested, keep records needed for follow up, or meet a legal obligation. It is not sold. Orynavo keeps it only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes.

Links to other sites

Other sites have their own privacy practices. This applies to every external link here, including LinkedIn. When you follow one, you leave the Orynavo website.

If this changes

Orynavo will update this notice if the site starts collecting information or uses a new service. This version took effect in September 2026.
