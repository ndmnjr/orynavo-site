Privacy

Plain by design.

This website is informational. It is deliberately simple and collects no visitor data through tracking tools.

What happens now

No analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies are used on this site. There are no account forms, newsletter forms, or contact forms here. Orynavo does not receive personal information simply because you visit these pages.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process routine technical logs—such as an IP address, browser information, and request time—for security and service operation under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email contact

If you email hello@orynavo.com, Orynavo receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, role, organisation, and message.

That information is used only to respond, conduct a requested research conversation, maintain necessary follow-up records, or meet legal obligations. It is not sold. Retention is limited to what is reasonably needed for those purposes.

External links

Links to third-party sites, including LinkedIn, are governed by those sites’ own privacy practices. Following one leaves the Orynavo website.

Changes

This notice will be revised if the site begins collecting information or uses new services. The current version is effective September 2026.
