Privacy

How this site handles your privacy.

This website offers information and, on the PhotonBid and LabWatch pages, paid plans through Dodo Payments. PhotonBid, LabWatch and Orynavo Retention for Whop use limited first party aggregate analytics; other pages do not use analytics.

When you visit

This site uses no advertising trackers or cookies. The only exception is the one sign in cookie on the account host account.orynavo.com (see your account). It has no newsletter. The plans on the PhotonBid and LabWatch pages have a request form that opens your email app, and the Create your account buttons lead to the account host, from which you go to the checkout of Dodo Payments (see plans, payment and the request form). The MarkerRank page links to the MarkerRank app, which has its own accounts and its own privacy notice. Only the PhotonBid and LabWatch product and tender pages and the Orynavo Retention for Whop page use the aggregate analytics described below; other pages, including this one, the terms and the refund pages, have no analytics.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may process routine technical logs, including your IP address, browser information and request time, to keep the service running and secure. You can read more in GitHub's privacy statement.

PhotonBid aggregate analytics

From 17 October 2026, the PhotonBid language pages send fixed event names to Orynavo's existing server at control.orynavo.com/photonbid/events, separate from our private CRM. We also record only the fixed page-language value English (en), French (fr) or German (de), not your browser language, nationality or location. Older events are English. We keep only UTC-day totals for page loads, selected sample/profile and video-link clicks, video starts and completions, and test or heuristically detected bot traffic. There is no public report-link event in use.

There are no cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. Our collector does not store raw IP addresses, user-agent strings, referrers, full URLs, query strings, email contents or contact details. The ingestion route has request logging disabled. IP addresses necessarily pass through network infrastructure; this is not a promise that hosting or network providers process no technical data. GitHub's routine hosting logs described above are separate.

Daily aggregates are retained for 90 UTC calendar days, with automatic daily deletion and no analytics database backups. We respect browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals by not sending these events. Blocking scripts or losing connectivity may prevent counts. Events are deduplicated only within the current page document; reloads count again. These are not unique visitors, sessions, verified humans, messages sent, leads or sales. Bot filtering is heuristic and other traffic is unverified. Marked QA events are stored separately and excluded from ordinary activity reports.

Page views and button clicks on product and tender pages

From 23 November 2026, our own server counts page views and button clicks on the PhotonBid and LabWatch product pages, the plans and sample alert pages and the tender pages as anonymous daily totals, without cookies, identifiers or IP addresses. It respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: when either signal is on, nothing is sent. Links in our emails may carry a campaign tag that contains no personal data.

Each count has only four fields: the UTC day, one of two fixed event names (page view or button click), the page address without any query string, and a button name such as the email or subscribe button. If the link you followed carries a campaign tag made of 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, for example a mailing label, we store that tag as one more field next to the count; anything else in the address is discarded. We do not store IP addresses, user agent strings, referrers or any visitor identifier, and nothing is written to your browser. The mailto buttons on these pages add a source label such as [src-seo-lasers] to the subject line of the email you choose to write, so that we can tell which page a message came from; you can delete it before sending. Counts go to the same collector as the PhotonBid analytics above (control.orynavo.com/photonbid/events), are kept for 90 UTC calendar days and then deleted automatically, and are event totals, not unique visitors, sessions, leads or sales. The legal pages and this page are not counted.
