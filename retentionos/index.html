Retention workflow for Whop businesses

Recover failed renewals without losing control of the message.

Orynavo Retention for Whop is a pre-launch workflow that detects recoverable subscription payment problems, prepares the right intervention and shows what happened next.

The initial focus is narrow: failed renewals and payments that require member action. It is not a live Whop integration yet, and it does not promise that every failed payment can be recovered.

The operating gap

A failed renewal becomes a retention problem when nobody owns the next step.

Whop already manages the payment and membership lifecycle. The proposed product adds a controlled operating layer around recoverable failures.

Signal

Know why action is needed.

Separate a payment that needs member authentication from a decline with no usable recovery path.

Intervention

Match the response to the event.

Prepare a useful message, suppress duplicates and keep the merchant in control of automatic outreach.

Outcome

Count only what can be defended.

Connect later payment outcomes to the intervention without calling every renewal recovered revenue.

Proposed first workflow

Detect, decide, deliver, measure.

The first version is designed around one auditable state machine rather than a broad marketing automation suite.

  1. Ingest the event once

    Verify the signed webhook, store its ID and ignore duplicate deliveries.

  2. Classify the recovery path

    Use the payment status, decline details and availability of a Whop recovery URL. Do not invent a link or retry a charge.

  3. Apply merchant rules

    Check suppression windows, contact limits, member status and whether the workflow requires approval.

  4. Prepare the intervention

    Start with Whop-native notifications where permissions and recipient eligibility are confirmed. Other channels remain optional and separately consented.

  5. Attribute the result

    Wait for a later payment outcome, preserve the timeline and show the merchant exactly why a result was counted.

What is verified today

The platform primitives exist. The product and distribution still need validation.

These boundaries reflect current public Whop documentation reviewed on 23 October 2026.

Verified

Realtime payment and membership webhooks. Whop documents failed payments, payments requiring action, successful payments, membership updates and trial-ending events.

Verified

Recovery information on eligible payments. A payment can expose a Whop recovery URL when action is available and the app has the required access.

Verified

Targeted Whop notifications. Apps can send notifications to eligible users with the required permission and an installed app relationship.

Planned

Merchant-approved recovery automation. Rules, templates, suppression and attribution are the proposed Orynavo product layer. They are not represented as live.

Not assumed

Email, SMS or unrestricted direct messaging. External channels require their own lawful basis, consent, provider setup and technical verification.

Build threshold3 design partners required

The product moves beyond a demonstrator only if qualified Whop operators confirm recurring failed-renewal volume, provide a measurable baseline and accept a controlled workflow test.

Controls before scale

Automation should be bounded, explainable and reversible.

The MVP specification puts operational safety ahead of message volume.

Approval modes

Review every intervention, approve a rule, or pause a workflow without deleting its history.

Rate and suppression limits

One active recovery sequence per payment problem, contact caps and immediate suppression after resolution.

Least privilege

Request only the Whop permissions needed for the enabled workflow and degrade clearly when optional access is declined.

Audit trail

Keep event IDs, rule decisions, approvals, message versions and outcome logic visible to the merchant.
