Questions

How are microscopy tenders in Poland published?

Buyers in Poland above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

What CPV code do microscope tenders use?

CPV 38510000 and the 3851 family (microscopes) are common, with 38515 for confocal and similar. Some are classified under laboratory or medical equipment.
