Questions

How are spectrometers tenders in Romania published?

Buyers in Romania above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

Which CPV code covers spectrometers?

CPV 38433000 (spectrometers) and its subcodes are typical, with 38432000 for analysis apparatus. Some are under 38000000 (laboratory equipment).
