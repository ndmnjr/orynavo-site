The service

LabWatch sends you matched tender alerts: open public tender notices that fit your laboratory catalogue and laboratory categories, as a daily or weekly digest, with the buyer, lot, deadline and a link to the official notice, and remarks on what a notice leaves open. Plans that include a bid pack add, for each opportunity you select, a short summary of the key requirements, the unknowns and the possible deal breakers. Sources are public notices: EU notices on TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), and for the EU and UK plans also UK notices on Find a Tender and Contracts Finder. EU notices are reused from TED (© European Union, source acknowledged). UK notices contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

What the service is not

The service is nonexclusive: the notices are public, so other suppliers can see the same ones, and we may send similar alerts to other customers. We do not guarantee that you will win, or be shortlisted for, any tender, and we do not guarantee full coverage: sources can be late or incomplete and some tenders are never published. Alerts and bid packs are screening aids based on public notices. They are not legal, procurement or bidding advice. The official notice and the buyer's tender documents always take precedence, and the decision to bid and the bid are yours.
