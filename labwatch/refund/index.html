Refund policy: LabWatch

Who refunds you

Your LabWatch subscription is bought from Dodo Payments, the reseller and merchant of record, so payments, invoices and refunds are handled under Dodo Payments' buyer terms (dodopayments.com/legal/buyer-terms). At the time of writing those terms leave refunds to Dodo Payments' discretion, case by case, and do not refund unused subscription periods. Nothing in this page limits a refund right that Dodo Payments or the law grants you.

Our position

There is no free trial, so please look at the sample alert on the plans section before you subscribe. If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after a cancellation took effect, email nadim@orynavo.com with the email address used to purchase and the order number. We will check it and ask Dodo Payments to refund the amount that was charged in error. If we end the service without your fault, we will refund the unused part of the paid period at our discretion, case by case.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period, you keep the service until then, and no further charge is made. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period.

Fully refunded or disputed payments

If the latest payment is fully refunded, or a payment dispute (chargeback) is lost or accepted, paid access ends immediately. Please contact us before opening a dispute, so that we can solve the problem.

Business customers

LabWatch is sold to businesses only, so the consumer right of withdrawal does not apply. See the terms.
