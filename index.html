01 / The problem

Most operational friction is not a software shortage. It is a decision problem with systems attached.

Before adding automation, we find where context gets lost, where judgment matters and what evidence a responsible person needs. The system follows that logic, not the latest tool.

02 / Capabilities

Where we help.

Focused work for teams carrying operational complexity, expensive decisions or a promising idea that still needs proof.

  1. 01

    Applied AI and agents

    Design assistants and agent workflows around real responsibilities. We define the evidence, permissions, review points and failure paths before automation is trusted with more work.

  2. 02

    Operational and decision systems

    Turn scattered spreadsheets, approvals and handoffs into a coherent control layer. The result may be a workflow, decision model, internal tool or integration that makes ownership visible.

  3. 03

    Telecom and asset lifecycle insight

    Frame problems across inventory, capital work, asset governance and operational data. We connect business rules to system behavior without pretending the underlying domain is simple.

  4. 04

    Validation and prototyping

    Test a service, internal system or product idea before a large build. Interviews, workflow traces and small prototypes reveal whether the problem is real and what a useful first version needs to do.

03 / Client triggers

The moment to bring us in.

A polished brief is not required. A consequential problem, access to the people doing the work and willingness to examine the current process are enough to begin.

  • A critical process lives across inboxes, spreadsheets and individual memory.
  • Your team has AI pilots, but no clear operating model for using them safely.
  • Leaders see reports, yet still cannot trace why a decision was made.
  • An internal tool idea needs evidence before budget and engineering time are committed.
  • Asset or project data exists, but people do not trust it enough to act.
04 / Approach

From ambiguity to a working system.

01 / Frame

Define the decision

Name the actor, the decision, the current evidence and the cost of getting it wrong.

02 / Trace

Follow the work

Observe the process as it happens. Find hidden handoffs, exceptions and points where context disappears.

03 / Build

Make the logic tangible

Create a small working system around the most important flow, with evidence and human review built in.

04 / Learn

Test in real work

Use real cases, record failure modes and decide what deserves further investment.

PhotonBid / Early research

Technical tender fit, traced to the source.

PhotonBid explores whether a specialist supplier can compare official European tender requirements with its actual catalogue before spending hours on a bid pack. Each possible fit would quote the requirement, show the product evidence and flag what could rule the opportunity out.

PhotonBid is a research project, not a released product. It does not write bids, give procurement advice or guarantee relevance, eligibility or outcomes.

06 / Strengths and boundaries

Useful where the work is messy and accountability still matters.

Orynavo is strong at

  • Translating operations into system logic without flattening the exceptions.
  • Connecting business owners and technical teams around a testable definition of useful.
  • Building evidence into AI workflows so people can inspect how an answer was reached.
  • Reducing the size of the bet through prototypes that answer a specific question.

Orynavo does not offer

  • Generic transformation programmes built around a predetermined platform.
  • Unattended automation where errors can create serious operational or financial harm.
  • Legal, regulatory or procurement advice. Qualified specialists remain responsible for those decisions.
  • Guaranteed outcomes. The work produces evidence and a working path, not certainty.
