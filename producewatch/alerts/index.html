How it works

Three steps, no account

1. You say what you buy

One commodity, one pack, and the terminal market or shipping point you watch.

2. You get one short email a week

The latest USDA price, the previous one, the change, and the USDA wording.

3. You decide after 14 days

It is free for 14 days. If it is useful, say yes and continue at USD 29 per month. If not, do nothing and it stops.

Honest limits

What this is, and what it is not

  • All figures come from public USDA AMS Market News reports. ProduceWatch adds no data of its own.
  • USDA publishes free reports and a free My Market News account that can email you a report. ProduceWatch only narrows that to the one line you care about and keeps the previous price next to it.
  • Terminal and shipping point prices are not delivered quotes. Freight, brand, credit and contract terms are not included.
  • USDA may revise or skip a report. If a report is late, the alert says so and shows the date it used.
  • This is a reference, not a forecast, advice or a guarantee of any outcome.
