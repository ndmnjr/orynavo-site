Read the comparison in context

A reference, not your delivered price.

The same reported specification is compared on both dates. Organic status is not reported for any of these items. Other missing fields remain unknown. Matching these source rows does not establish a match to your purchase specification.

Freight, service, quality, volume, credit, timing and contract terms can explain differences from supplier quotes. Your team retains the purchasing decision. A missing or incompatible observation should have no calculated movement, rather than a substituted product or pack.

How the price movement is calculated

Midpoint = (reported low + high) / 2. Change = October 2 midpoint minus October 1 midpoint. Percent = change / October 1 midpoint × 100, rounded half up to one decimal. A single reported price uses the same low and high.

We use the full reported range, not the narrower “mostly” range. There is no unit conversion. Midpoints are calculations, not transaction averages. Two dates alone do not establish a trend or a customer match.
