How it works

Start with what
you actually buy.

A comparison needs more than an item name. Pack, size, variety, grade, origin and market all matter.

Not reported means not known. The sample does not establish organic status or customer-specific equivalence. Freight, quality, volume and contract terms can explain differences from your quote.

  1. Set the watchlist

    Share your regularly purchased items and pack sizes. We confirm the available sources and comparison scope.

  2. Compare the same specification

    Check matching source observations across report dates. Keep the original price range and identify missing fields.

  3. Deliver for your review

    Receive the agreed report by email, or discuss a system feed. Your team retains the purchasing decision.
