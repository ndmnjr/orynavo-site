Can I compare it directly with a delivered quote?

Not without checking the full basis. Specification, unit, currency, date, freight, quality, volume and contract terms all matter. A shipping-point reference does not establish your delivered price, a fair-price verdict or savings. Your team retains the buying and selling decisions.

What do the existing samples cover?

The film covers one dated broccoli price observation and explicitly shows missing history, movement and correction evidence. The separate Baltimore sample compares four source specifications on October 1 and 2, 2026. Neither is a customer-matched basket or a live feed.
