About Orynavo

Small, focused software tools that do one job well, run by one independent operator.

What Orynavo does

Orynavo builds small, focused software tools that do one job well. Each product is built for one kind of team and one recurring job.

Orynavo does not build platforms. Every product starts from one job a specific team already does by hand, and stays small enough to do that job well. Every product says plainly what it does and what it does not, and a person stays in control of any consequential decision.

Who runs it

Orynavo is run by Nadim Najjar, its founder, as one independent operator. He is also the publication director named in the legal notice. Questions about a product go to him directly, by email (see Contact below).

Each product has its own privacy notice: the Orynavo site privacy page (which also covers PhotonBid and LabWatch), the MarkerRank privacy notice and the ProduceWatch alerts privacy notice.
