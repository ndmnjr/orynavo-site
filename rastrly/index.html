See where your clients rank on Google Maps, street by street.

Rastrly is a Google Maps rank tracker for local SEO agencies and freelancers. It checks a grid of points around a business and shows the position at each one, then turns the result into a white-label PDF in your client's language.

How it works

Three steps, no credits to track.

01

Pick a business and keyword

Add a client, the search term and the area to cover.

02

Run a grid scan

We query a licensed search data provider from each grid point, so the position reflects where the searcher stands.

03

Send the report

Get a heat map, a short written summary with suggested next steps (generated automatically, review before sending) and a white-label PDF in English, German or Spanish.
