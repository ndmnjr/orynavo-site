Geo grid rank tracker comparison: five tools, public facts, dated

By the MarkerRank team · Last verified 2026-11-24

We read the public pages of five geo-grid tools on 2026-11-24. Whitespark has the lowest entry price, Local Falcon the lowest entry price per point, and BrightLocal bundles grids into a platform. MarkerRank costs more per point but adds EU hosting and reports in four languages. Places Scout is no longer sold separately.

How to choose

  • Lowest cost per grid point: Local Falcon, then Whitespark, at the entry plans above.
  • Grids plus citations, reviews and listings in one account: BrightLocal.
  • Larger grids, scheduling, an API and AI visibility reports: Local Falcon and Whitespark both list scheduling and an API; Local Falcon also lists AI visibility tracking.
  • Reports in English, German, Spanish or French, an EU-hosted app and a card-free trial: MarkerRank, with the limits listed below.

Tool by tool

Local Falcon

Credit-based plans from USD 24.99 a month, scheduling, API, AI visibility tracking and grids to 21x21. White-label reports only on the Premium monthly plan. Our Local Falcon comparison goes through it row by row.

BrightLocal Local Search Grid

A grid feature inside a larger platform, priced per location. Strong if you also use citations and reviews. See the BrightLocal comparison.

Whitespark Local Ranking Grids

Credit plans from USD 10 a month, up to 225 grid points, rankings to position 100, daily, weekly or monthly scheduling and free migration from another tool on a paid plan. We have no dedicated comparison page for it yet.

Local Viking (LocalOptics)

A Google Business Profile management tool with geogrids. Its pricing link leads to a LocalOptics page with plans from USD 39 a month and white-label reporting from the Pro plan. We could not confirm how the two names relate beyond what those pages show.

Places Scout and GridRival

Left out on purpose. Places Scout was acquired by Yext (announced 10 February 2025) and its domain now redirects to yext.com, with no public pricing found. We found no geo-grid tool called GridRival.

MarkerRank

Grid scans, heat maps and white-label PDFs in four languages, from EUR 29 a month, hosted in Helsinki, Finland. Start the free trial.

Questions

Which geo-grid rank tracker has the lowest public price?

On 2026-11-24 Whitespark Local Ranking Grids lists the lowest entry plan in this table: USD 10 a month for 2,000 credits. Local Falcon Starter is USD 24.99 for 7,500 credits, which is a lower price per point (about USD 0.0033 against USD 0.005). Prices are in US dollars on those sites and change.

Is Places Scout still available?

Not as a separate product. Yext announced the acquisition of Places Scout on 10 February 2025, and placesscout.com now redirects to yext.com, where the related product is called Scout by Yext. We could not find public pricing, so it is not in the table.

Is there a tool called GridRival?

We found no geo-grid rank tracker with that name. Searches for the word return an F1 fantasy game, so we did not include it.
