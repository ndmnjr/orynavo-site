This page

This page sets no cookies and loads no external fonts. GitHub Pages hosts it and, as hosting provider, may process routine technical logs such as IP addresses.

Statistics

We measure visits with GoatCounter, a cookieless, privacy-friendly statistics tool that we host ourselves on our own server in Helsinki, Finland. It sets no cookies, does not keep your IP address or any data that could identify you, and does not track you across sites. We only count page views and a few anonymous clicks (for example on the signup button). Campaign tags in a link (such as ?ref=youtube) are counted as the traffic source; other parts of the address are not sent. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). No consent banner is needed: nothing is stored on your device, your IP address is not kept and the statistics cannot identify you.

Video on this page

The 28-second video loads from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com, Google) only after you press its play button. Until then your browser makes no request to YouTube or Google for it. Once you press play, YouTube receives your IP address and technical data under its own privacy policy and may set cookies or similar storage. If you prefer not to, do not press play; the video is also on our YouTube channel.

Account and workspace data: to provide the service (performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). How long we keep it

Account and workspace data: while your account is active. After a trial or subscription ends, your data stays readable for 30 days and is then deleted; you can also delete it at any time (Workspace settings > Delete my account and data, or by email to nadim@orynavo.com). Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Server logs: web-server access logs 14 days; application logs up to 30 days. Signup-abuse records: 30 days, except the one-way hash of your mailbox, kept to enforce one free trial per mailbox. Subscription records received from Dodo Payments and API cost records: 10 years, as French accounting law requires (Code de commerce L123-22), kept separately from workspace data and no longer linked to you after your account is deleted.
