Privacy notice: MarkerRank waitlist

Who is responsible

Orynavo, contact nadim@orynavo.com. Orynavo's general privacy notice is at orynavo.com/privacy.html.

What we collect

Only what you send: your email address and, if you choose, how many clients you manage and what you use today. The MarkerRank page itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no external fonts. We also record the language you used.

Why and legal basis

To send you updates about MarkerRank's launch. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give by ticking the box and submitting the form.

How the sign-up reaches us

Submitting the form sends your answers to a private Google Form owned by Orynavo; responses are stored in a private Google Sheet that only Orynavo can open. Google (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) acts as our processor for this storage and may transfer data outside the EU under its standard contractual terms. If the connection fails, the page opens your email app with a prepared message to us instead. GitHub Pages hosts this page and may process routine technical logs.

How long we keep it

Until MarkerRank launches plus 6 months, or until you withdraw consent, whichever is first.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority. Email nadim@orynavo.com and write STOP to be removed; we delete your row from the sheet on request.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. Besides Google (storage) and GitHub (page hosting), nobody receives it.
