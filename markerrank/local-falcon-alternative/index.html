Local Falcon alternative for agencies that report in the client's language

By the MarkerRank team · Last verified 2026-11-25

MarkerRank is a smaller, grid-only alternative to Local Falcon for agencies that send reports in the client's language. It is not cheaper: on 2026-11-25 Local Falcon Starter lists USD 24.99 for 7,500 points a month, MarkerRank Solo EUR 29 for 5,000. Choose MarkerRank for EU hosting, a card-free trial and white-label PDFs on every plan.

Where Local Falcon is stronger

  • Scheduled scans, so rankings are tracked without anyone pressing a button.
  • An API, an MCP server and connectors for Looker Studio, Zapier and n8n.
  • AI visibility tracking across several AI search products.
  • Grids up to 21x21 and Apple Maps tracking, against 7x7 and Google Maps only here.
  • More points per unit of money at the entry plans.

Where MarkerRank may fit better

  • Reports in the client's language (English, German, Spanish or French) with your logo, on every plan including the trial.
  • The app is hosted in Helsinki, Finland, with a privacy notice written for GDPR. Local Falcon does not state its hosting location on the pages we read, so ask them if it matters to you.
  • A 14-day trial with no card, billed in euros, with a fixed monthly allowance. A scan that would go over it is not run and nothing extra is charged.
  • One job only: grid scans and the report. No suite to learn, and no sales call to get started.

What MarkerRank does not do yet

These limits are real today:

  • No scheduled scans yet: every scan is started by you.
  • Ranks beyond position 20 are not shown.
  • No API.
  • No AI visibility tracking (ChatGPT, Gemini and similar).
  • No mobile app.
  • Grid sizes on paid plans are 3x3, 5x5 and 7x7.

Who should stay with Local Falcon

Stay if you need scheduled scans, API access, AI visibility reports or very large grids, or if cost per point is your main concern. Consider MarkerRank if you mostly run monthly grid scans for clients who read French, German or Spanish, and want a plain report with your name on it.

Questions

Is MarkerRank cheaper than Local Falcon?

No. On 2026-11-25 Local Falcon Starter costs USD 24.99 for 7,500 credits a month, which is about USD 0.0033 per grid point. MarkerRank Solo costs EUR 29 for 5,000 points, about EUR 0.0058 per point. If price per point decides it for you, Local Falcon is the cheaper option.

Why would an agency choose MarkerRank over Local Falcon?

Three reasons that do not depend on price: reports in English, German, Spanish or French with your logo on every plan, app hosting in Helsinki, Finland, and a 14-day trial that needs no card. If you need scheduled scans, an API or AI visibility tracking, Local Falcon offers them and MarkerRank does not.

Does Local Falcon offer white-label reports?

Yes, on its Premium plan, USD 199.99 a month or USD 2,399.88 billed annually, according to the plan table read on 2026-11-25. The Starter, Basic and Pro plans show No for white-label reports, on both monthly and annual billing.

Does MarkerRank have scheduled scans or an API?

No. Scans are started by you, and there is no API yet. Local Falcon lists scheduled scans and an API.

Where is MarkerRank hosted and who runs it?

The app runs on Hetzner servers in Helsinki, Finland. MarkerRank is made by Orynavo, a French sole proprietorship (entrepreneur individuel) based in Paris. Details are in the legal notice and the privacy notice.
