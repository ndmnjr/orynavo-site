How billing works

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. You need a verified email address and nothing else. No card is asked for or stored.

When does billing start?

Only after you confirm a paid plan yourself. Nothing is charged automatically at the end of the trial.

What happens when the trial ends?

Scans stop. You can still open and read your data for 30 days.

What if I use up my monthly allowance?

A scan that would go over the allowance of your plan is not run, and nothing extra is charged.

Can I pay today?

Not yet. Paid plans open soon, see above. Prices are in euro per month; how VAT applies will be confirmed when paid plans open.
