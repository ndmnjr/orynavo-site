GMB Everywhere alternative for grid rank maps and client reports

By the MarkerRank team · Last verified 2026-11-25

Keep GMB Everywhere if you want cheap, unlimited profile audits inside Google Maps: its paid plan is $15 to $30 a month. Pick MarkerRank when you need what GMB Everywhere's pages do not list: a 49-point grid map of rankings, comparison with an earlier scan and a white-label PDF for clients. Most agencies would use both.

The two products overlap only on rank checking. GMB Everywhere checks one location per search; MarkerRank checks up to 49 at once and keeps the result for a client report.

What it costs to use both

Using the prices above, one agency user with GMB Everywhere Power User billed annually ($15 a month) and MarkerRank Solo (EUR 29 a month) pays $15 plus EUR 29 a month, before tax. Currencies are not converted. MarkerRank Solo covers 5 clients and 5,000 grid points a month, which is 102 scans at 7x7 or 200 at 5x5.

Where GMB Everywhere is stronger

  • Price: a free plan, and unlimited use from $15 a month billed annually.
  • It works inside Google Maps and Search, so competitor categories and audits appear while you browse.
  • Profile audits MarkerRank does not have: categories, services, attributes, reviews, posts, website vs profile.
  • AI tools for posts, review replies, descriptions and images, and an AI visibility checker.
  • Teleport checks a rank from any city or coordinate worldwide, without setting up a client first.

Where MarkerRank may fit better

  • You need to see rankings across the whole area around a business, not one spot: up to 49 points per scan, in about 45 seconds.
  • You send monthly proof to clients: comparison with an earlier scan, white-label PDF in English, German, Spanish or French, and a share link you can switch off.
  • You want an EU-hosted app (Helsinki, Finland) billed in euros, with a 14-day trial and no card.

What MarkerRank does not do yet

  • No scheduled scans yet: every scan is started by you.
  • Ranks beyond position 20 are not shown.
  • No API.
  • No profile audits or AI writing tools.
  • No browser extension.
  • Grid sizes on paid plans are 3x3, 5x5 and 7x7.

Add grid maps next to GMB Everywhere in 10 minutes

  1. Start the trial (no card).
  2. Add the client and its business.
  3. Enter a keyword the way customers type it, without the city name, and pick a 7x7 grid.
  4. Run the scan (about 45 seconds) and keep it as the client's before picture.
  5. Download the white-label PDF or copy the share link.

The 10 minutes is our estimate for one client and one keyword. For the full routine, see the weekly Google Business Profile checklist for agencies.

Who should stay with GMB Everywhere only

Stay if you mostly audit profiles and check ranks by hand, or if your clients do not ask for ranking reports. If you send monthly Google Maps reports, test MarkerRank on one client.

Questions

Does GMB Everywhere have a geo-grid rank tracker?

Not on the pages we read on 2026-11-25. Its Teleport feature checks a profile's rank for a keyword from one chosen location at a time. The homepage and pricing page do not mention grids or heat maps.

Is MarkerRank cheaper than GMB Everywhere?

No. GMB Everywhere Power User is $30 a month, or $15 a month billed annually, with unlimited use. MarkerRank starts at EUR 29 a month with a fixed grid point allowance. They do different jobs, so many agencies would use both.

Can MarkerRank replace GMB Everywhere?

Only for rank checking. MarkerRank has no profile audits, category finder, review or post audits, or AI writing tools. It adds 49-point grid maps, comparison with an earlier scan and white-label PDF reports.

Does GMB Everywhere make client reports?

We found no PDF, export or white-label report on its homepage or pricing page. Ask GMB Everywhere if you need one.
