Questions

Is there an online checkout?

Yes. Subscribe opens the secure Dodo Payments checkout. Dodo Payments is the merchant of record, sends the invoice and handles VAT. After payment we send your welcome email within one business day. Questions: nadim@orynavo.com.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, month to month. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.

Are the prices final?

Prices are monthly and exclude VAT, with no lock-in. Tender-file preparation is quoted per tender from EUR 1,500 and invoiced separately.

What is a matched alert?

An email listing open public notices that fit your product profile, with deadline, CPV code and a link to the original notice.

Do you guarantee that I will win a tender?

No. We help you find and assess notices; the bid decision and the bid are yours.
