About this product category

Optical spectrometers split light by wavelength to measure what a sample absorbs, emits or scatters. This page covers UV-Vis, FTIR, Raman and similar optical instruments. Mass spectrometers, ICP, NMR and EPR instruments are laboratory analytical equipment and are listed on LabWatch.

Buyers are university chemistry and physics groups, public laboratories and quality control services. They specify spectral range, resolution, detection limits, accessories, software compliance and service terms. CPV 38433 is the family usually used.

Not every purchase is public: direct awards and small buys do not appear on TED. Always check the original notice before deciding to bid.

What does winner of record mean?

It is the supplier named in the published award notice. A reseller can be the winner of record while supplying another maker's product.
