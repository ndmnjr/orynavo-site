About this product category

Laser tenders range from research systems (ultrafast, tunable, high power) to laser sources built into larger instruments such as microscopes or processing machines. CPV 31712 and 38636 are the codes most often used for laser equipment, but integrated systems are often classified under the host instrument.

Buyers typically ask for wavelength, output power or pulse energy, pulse duration, beam quality, stability, safety class and interlocks, cooling, and installation by the supplier. Many notices allow equivalent products.

Not every purchase is public: direct awards and small buys do not appear on TED. Always check the original notice before deciding to bid.

Questions

Where do these lasers notices come from?

Open notices come from TED (ted.europa.eu) and, where shown, national portals. Each row links to the original notice, which is the authoritative source.

What does winner of record mean?

It is the supplier named in the published award notice. A reseller can be the winner of record while supplying another maker's product.

Is this a price list or a forecast?

No. Values are as published in award notices, in the original currency, and 'value not published' means the notice shows none or a placeholder. Past awards do not predict future purchases.
