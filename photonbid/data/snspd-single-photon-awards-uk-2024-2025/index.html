Method and limits

We read published award records for superconducting nanowire single photon detectors (SNSPD) and other single photon detectors: TED (EU), Contracts Finder and Find a Tender (UK), and the national feeds we hold (Italy ANAC, Poland e-Zamowienia, Germany, Switzerland). Each row was opened on the official notice again on 24 Nov 2026 to confirm the notice type and the winner of record, and the link was checked. Values are shown as published, in the original currency. Approximate EUR figures use ECB reference rates of 20 Nov 2026 (DKK 7.4746, NOK 10.7180, GBP 0.83735 per EUR).

  • Coverage: published awards above the EU threshold on TED, plus the national feeds we hold. Below-threshold and unpublished purchases are missing.
  • Window: TED holds up to 36 months of awards (to 22 Nov 2026). National feeds are shorter: Germany about 400 days, Poland and Switzerland 365 days, the UK feed about one year, the Italian register from 2024. Counts mix these windows, so they are not a count for the same period in every country.
  • History, not forecast. A past award does not mean the buyer will buy again.
  • Dates are notice publication dates.
  • The winner of record is not always the manufacturer. Resellers can supply another maker's product.
  • Some notices publish no value, or a placeholder such as EUR 0.01. Those rows show 'value not published', never zero.
  • Small samples. Treat ranges as orientation, not a price list.
  • Rows dropped on purpose: X-ray photon counting, CT, multiphoton microscopy, cameras, single photon sources, foundry work, unrelated lots.
  • UK: Contracts Finder was checked live; Find a Tender only for one known notice. Contracts Finder holds a limited share of university purchases.
