Questions

How are analytical instruments tenders in France published?

Buyers in France above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

What do analytical instrument tenders usually include?

Besides the instrument, notices often include installation, training, a warranty period and sometimes consumables or service for several years.
