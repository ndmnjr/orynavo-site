Questions

How are lasers tenders in Italy published?

Buyers in Italy above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

Which CPV codes are used for laser tenders?

Common ones are 38636100 (lasers, other than laser diodes) and the 31712 family, but laser components often sit in laboratory or measurement codes. Check the full notice text and lot descriptions, not only the CPV.
