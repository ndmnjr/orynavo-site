Questions

What is this page?

A directory of public tender categories for specialist suppliers (lasers, optics, microscopy, analytical and laboratory equipment, measurement). Each category page lists currently open notices from TED with deadlines and links to the original notice.

How current is the list?

Pages are regenerated from our database; this version was generated on 21 Nov 2026. Only notices whose deadline is still in the future are listed.

What is public procurement and how do tenders work?

Public procurement is how governments, hospitals and universities buy goods and services. Above certain value thresholds the buyer must publish a notice (in the EU on TED) with the subject, CPV code, deadline and where to get the tender documents; suppliers then submit offers by the deadline.

How do I set up tender alerts?

You can save searches on TED yourself, which is free. If you prefer matched notices filtered to your products and delivered by email, write to nadim@orynavo.com; plans start at EUR 99 per month ex VAT.

How can I get alerts for these tenders by email?

Email nadim@orynavo.com with a few lines about what you sell and which countries you serve. We reply with the open notices that match, and you can then decide whether to subscribe. Plans start at EUR 99 per month ex VAT, no lock-in.

Is this legal advice or a guarantee of winning?

No. The pages summarise public notices to help you find them. Always check the original notice and tender documents, and decide yourself whether to bid. We do not guarantee any award.
