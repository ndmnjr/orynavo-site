How are optical instruments tenders in Poland published?

Buyers in Poland above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

Why is this category broad?

Notices use CPV 386 and 3863 codes loosely. Because the match here is partly by CPV code, always check that the lot description matches what you supply.
