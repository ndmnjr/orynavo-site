Questions

How are laboratory equipment tenders in Finland published?

Buyers in Finland above the EU thresholds publish on TED, usually in the national language, and often also on a national e-procurement portal where the tender documents are available. Each row below links to the TED notice.

Do laboratory tenders allow bidding on single lots?

Most multi-lot tenders do, but some require bidding on complete groups. The procurement documents define this.
