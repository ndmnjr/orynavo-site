How to find photonics tenders on TED and national portals

Photonics tenders above the EU threshold are published on TED, the EU's procurement journal. UK tenders are on Find a Tender and Contracts Finder. Smaller purchases sit on national portals, one per country. To catch lasers, detectors and optics, search by CPV code and by title keywords in the buyer's language. Today's open notices by product are on our photonics tender pages.

One clarification first: EU research funding calls are also called "tenders" on the EU Funding and Tenders Portal. This guide covers public purchases, where a lab or institute buys equipment and you bid to supply it.

Where are photonics tenders published?

It depends on value and country. EU rules require publication on TED for supplies from EUR 140,000 (central government) or EUR 216,000 (sub-central authorities) in 2026 and 2027. Below that, each country publishes on its own platform, or not at all.

How to search TED for photonics tenders

TED is free to search without an account. An account adds saved searches and alerts. You can save up to 25 search profiles and set up to 25 search alerts, daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with an RSS feed for each saved search.

Step 1: pick CPV codes. Photonics products sit under 38000000, laboratory, optical and precision equipment. The narrower codes include 38600000 (optical instruments), 38636000 (specialist optical instruments) and its child 38636100 (lasers). Start broad, then narrow once you see the volume.

Step 2: add title keywords in several languages. CPV codes alone let noise in. In multi-lot notices, every lot shares the notice's CPV list, so a code filter can return a lot for vehicles next to the one for a spectrometer. We match on titles for this reason. A German buyer writes "Laser" or "Spektrometer", a Polish one "spektrometr", an Italian one "rivelatore". For single photon detection, include "SNSPD" and "single photon" plus the local terms.

Step 3: read the notice type. A contract notice opens the bid window. A contract award notice names the winner and often the value, which tells you who sells to that buyer.

Step 4: save it as an alert. One alert per product family keeps the inbox readable.

UK: Find a Tender, Contracts Finder and Public Contracts Scotland

UK buyers left TED on 1 January 2021, when Find a Tender replaced it for high-value contracts. GOV.UK describes Find a Tender as the service for contracts usually above GBP 139,688 including VAT, and Contracts Finder as the place for contracts over GBP 12,000 with central government and its agencies. Scotland has its own portal, Public Contracts Scotland.

University purchases in our data appear on both. The University of Sheffield's SNSPD system for GBP 140,157 is on Contracts Finder. See the UK single photon award list for more.

National portals: Italy, Poland, Germany and Switzerland

  • Italy. Awards are published on ANAC's legal publicity platform. Supplies under EUR 140,000 can be directly awarded, so most Italian lab purchases appear only as awards. Our article on direct awards in Italy shows six real examples.
  • Poland. e-Zamówienia is the free national platform run by the Public Procurement Office. The Institute of Physics PAN bought a single photon detector array for EUR 55,200 there (2026/BZP 00495060).
  • Germany. service.bund.de lists tenders from federal, state and municipal authorities, with newsletters and RSS feeds. Larger German purchases also reach TED: 11 of the 34 core SNSPD awards in our data are German.
  • Switzerland. simap.ch is the Swiss public procurement platform, used by the Confederation and other public buyers to publish tenders.

What a tender search misses

Keyword alerts find open tenders. They miss three things that often matter more for a photonics supplier:

  1. Negotiated awards without a call. Some buyers argue that only one supplier can deliver and publish only the award. You see the order after it is placed.
  2. Direct awards below national limits. In Italy, six of six SNSPD and single photon purchases from July to November 2026 went by direct or negotiated award.
  3. Buyer history. Who bought, from whom, and when the contract ends. The SNSPD contract winner map shows that kind of history for the awards in our data.

Our guide on how public labs buy SNSPD systems explains when each route applies.

A weekly routine

If you run the search yourself, this keeps it to one sitting a week:

  • Open your TED alerts, Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, and mark anything with a deadline in the next 30 days.
  • Check the national portals for your priority countries.
  • Read the award notices in your category and note the winners and values.
  • Log each buyer that bought from a competitor. That list is next year's pipeline.

FAQ

Is TED free to use?

Yes. Searching and reading notices on TED needs no account. A free account adds saved searches, email alerts and RSS feeds.

Which CPV code covers lasers?

38636100, lasers, under 38636000 specialist optical instruments. Buyers do not always pick the narrowest code, so combine codes with title keywords.

Are UK tenders still on TED?

No. Since 1 January 2021, new UK procurements publish on Find a Tender for high-value contracts and on Contracts Finder for lower values, with Public Contracts Scotland for Scottish buyers.
