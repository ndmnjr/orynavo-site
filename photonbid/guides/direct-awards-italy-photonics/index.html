Direct awards in Italy: why most photonics deals never become a tender

In Italy, a public buyer can award a supply contract worth less than EUR 140,000 directly to one supplier, without a call for competition, under article 50 of D.Lgs. 36/2023. Most lab instruments, detectors and service contracts cost less than that. So in Italy the purchase often happens before you would ever see a tender. The Italian single photon award list shows what this looks like in practice.

What is a direct award in Italy?

A direct award (affidamento diretto) is a contract the buyer gives to a supplier of its choice without a tender procedure. Article 50(1)(b) allows it for services and supplies below EUR 140,000, even without consulting several suppliers, as long as the chosen supplier has documented, relevant experience.

The buyer may ask for more than one quote. That does not turn the purchase into a tender. The award is still published afterwards, as the ANAC records below show.

Compare that with the EU threshold. For 2026 and 2027, EU rules apply to supplies from EUR 140,000 for central government and EUR 216,000 for sub-central authorities. Between EUR 140,000 and the EU threshold, art. 50 sets a negotiated procedure with invited suppliers instead.

Why lab purchases stay under the line

Two things push Italian lab purchases into direct awards, in our reading of the records:

  • Prices. A single photon detector, a counting module or a year of cryogenic service costs tens of thousands of euros, not hundreds of thousands. The six awards above prove the point.
  • Specification. The researcher often knows which instrument fits the experiment. A direct award lets the buyer pick that supplier, with reasons on file.

Above EUR 140,000 the picture changes. The two larger Italian purchases in our data, a SPAD array detector at IIT for EUR 168,374.76 (TED 71369-2026) and a multi wavelength detector with TCSPC electronics at CNR for EUR 429,930 (TED 748359-2025), both went through open procedures on TED.

What the rotation rule changes for suppliers

Italy limits repeat awards to the same supplier. Under article 49 of D.Lgs. 36/2023, a buyer may not give a new contract to the outgoing contractor when two consecutive awards fall in the same product or service sector. The buyer can make an exception with reasons, for example when the market has no real alternative, and direct awards below EUR 5,000 are exempt.

For a challenger, rotation is an opening: the lab that bought from your competitor last time may need a second name next time. For an incumbent, it means your next order at the same buyer may need a written justification on their side.

Where direct awards become visible

Italian awards are published on ANAC's legal publicity platform, which is where the six links above point. Each record names the buyer, the winner, the object and the value. The catch: you see the award after the order. For finding open opportunities on TED and other portals, see how to find photonics tenders on TED and national portals.

How to sell into Italy when there is no tender

You cannot bid on a direct award. You can be the supplier the researcher already has in mind. That takes three habits:

  1. Read the award history by buyer. Which institutes bought detectors, from whom and at what price. The SNSPD contract winner map shows Italy is split between several suppliers, with no single leader.
  2. Time your visit. A service contract that renews each year, or a lab that bought a system years ago, tells you when a conversation is due.
  3. Make the quote easy to justify. Give the buyer a clear technical reason to choose you, written so it fits a direct award decision.

FAQ

What is the direct award threshold in Italy?

For services and supplies, EUR 140,000 (excluding VAT), under article 50(1)(b) of D.Lgs. 36/2023. For works it is EUR 150,000.

Can a foreign supplier win an Italian direct award?

Yes. The law asks for a supplier with documented, relevant experience, not an Italian one. In our data, ID Quantique and Single Quantum won three of the six Italian awards between them.

Where can I find Italian direct awards?

On ANAC's legal publicity platform, which publishes award results with buyer, winner and value. They do not appear on TED unless the value passes the EU threshold.
