Your supplier profile

A keyword cannot tell whether you should bid.

PhotonBid uses the commercial and delivery constraints behind a real bid decision, not a loose list of search terms.

  • What you sellProduct families, catalogue specifications, supported configurations and related services.
  • Where you competeTarget markets, buyer types, regions and distributor territories.
  • What you can deliverDeal-size limits, service coverage, installation capacity and required certifications.
  • When you do not bidNo-bid rules such as excluded sectors, unsupported obligations, unsuitable contract terms or missing partner coverage.

A review brief, not another alert

See the case for review before opening the bid pack.

PhotonBid compares live public tenders with the supplier profile and gives your team a short, traceable first review.

  1. Source evidence

    Quoted requirements and a link back to the official notice or document.

  2. Likely catalogue fit

    The product family or service that appears relevant, with the reasoning kept visible.

  3. Unknowns and deal breakers

    Missing information, eligibility concerns and requirements that may rule the opportunity out.

  4. Review priority

    A practical way to sort the opportunities that need human attention first.

A confidence score is a triage score. It helps order a review queue. It is not a literal percentage fit, a guarantee of eligibility or a prediction of winning.

Decision boundary

Your team makes the bid or no-bid decision.

PhotonBid is designed to help people decide where to look. It does not replace commercial, technical or procurement judgment.

  • The official notice always takes precedence. Check the source for scope, deadlines, value, amendments, eligibility and submission requirements.
  • Coverage and detection are not guaranteed. Public sources can change, documents can be incomplete and relevant opportunities can be missed.
  • PhotonBid does not write or submit bids. It does not provide legal or procurement advice and does not predict awards.
