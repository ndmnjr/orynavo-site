How it could work

See why a tender might fit your catalogue.

  1. Read the official requirements

    Start with the tender notice and its source documents. Pull out requirements for the equipment, configuration, service and supplier eligibility.

  2. Compare them with the actual catalogue

    Check the product families and the specifications that matter for the tender. These may include technique, wavelength, spectral range, sensitivity, resolution, accessories and service capability. A broad category match is not enough.

  3. Explain the possible fit and deal breakers

    Show the quoted evidence and the possible product match. Flag unclear or disqualifying requirements, along with the deadline, value, eligibility concerns and a link to the official source. Your team can then check the notice directly.

Limits

Your team still makes the decision.

  • PhotonBid would not write or submit bids. It would help your team find opportunities that may deserve a closer look.
  • It would not provide legal or procurement advice. Your team remains responsible for interpretation, eligibility and every decision.
  • It could make mistakes or miss information. Relevance, completeness, eligibility and outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
  • The official notice always takes precedence. Check the official source for deadlines, value, scope, amendments and requirements before acting.
